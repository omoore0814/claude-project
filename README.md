## Our Services

### Injectables

- **Botox & Neuromodulators** — Smooth fine lines and dynamic wrinkles. Also used to address migraines, jaw pain, and excessive sweating (hyperhidrosis).
- **Dermal Fillers** — Restore lost volume, contour facial features, and plump lips and cheeks for a refreshed, youthful appearance. Results can last up to 2 years with no surgery required.
- **Belkyra (Chin Fat Removal)** — An injectable treatment using deoxycholic acid to safely and effectively target stubborn areas of fullness under the chin.
- **Skinvive** — Hyaluronic acid-based injections to deeply hydrate skin, improve glow, texture, and overall smoothness.

### Skin & Face Treatments

- **Morpheus8 RF Microneedling** — Stimulate collagen production, tighten skin, and improve texture using innovative radiofrequency microneedling technology for face and body.
- **Emface** — A needle-free, non-surgical facial rejuvenation treatment that lifts, tones, and smooths facial contours using a combination of electromagnetic stimulation and radiofrequency.
- **Face Scanning & Analysis** — Advanced skin analysis to help create customized, data-driven treatment plans.
- **Prescription Skincare Products** — Professional-grade skincare lines including SkinMedica, AlumierMD, and Latisse for comprehensive at-home care between treatments.

### Body Contouring & Wellness

- **Emsculpt NEO** — Build muscle and reduce fat simultaneously via advanced electromagnetic stimulation. Effective for the abdomen, thighs, buttocks, and more — no surgery, no downtime.
- **Emsella** — Strengthen the pelvic floor with a non-invasive treatment that effectively addresses urinary incontinence and supports sexual wellness.
- **PRP Hair Restoration** — Use your body's own platelet-rich plasma (PRP) to stimulate hair growth and restore density naturally.
