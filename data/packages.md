## Package Design Notes
- Packages should include at least 2 complementary services
- Discount should feel meaningful (15–25%) without destroying margin
- Always recommend a package over single sessions when client needs a series
- Name packages after outcomes, not service names (e.g. "Body Reboot" not "Emsculpt + Cavitation")
