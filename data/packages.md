# Packages — Medical Esthetics Thunder Bay

> Last updated: 2026-03-20
> Packages are named after outcomes, not services. Clients buy transformation, not treatments.
> Target: packages should represent 25–30% of total monthly revenue.

---

## Package Design Rules
- Minimum 2 complementary services per package
- Discount 15–20% vs. individual pricing (meaningful savings without destroying margin)
- Always name after the outcome or feeling the client wants
- Always pair with a retail product recommendation
- Review performance quarterly — retire slow sellers, promote top sellers

## Package Sales Conversation Triggers

Suggest a package when a client:
- Books their second session of the same treatment ("You're already loving the results — would you like to lock in your next two at a savings?")
- Mentions multiple concerns in one consultation
- Asks "how many sessions will I need?"
- Has recently had a life event (new baby, weight loss, birthday, upcoming event)
- Asks about maintenance ("A package locks in your results at the best price")

## Package Upsell Script

> "Based on what you're looking to achieve, I'd actually recommend our [Package Name] — it combines [brief description] into one complete plan. A lot of our clients see the best results this way, and it saves you [savings amount] compared to booking each session individually. Would you like me to walk you through what's included?"
