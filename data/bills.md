## Bill Review SOP (Quick Reference)

**1st of the month:**
- [ ] Log into bank — confirm all auto-drafts from prior month processed correctly
- [ ] Review bills due in the next 15 days
- [ ] Pay any manual bills due before the 15th
- [ ] Update Status column for all reviewed bills
- [ ] Log totals in Monthly Bill Summary

**15th of the month:**
- [ ] Confirm all bills paid in first half of month
- [ ] Review bills due before month end
- [ ] Flag any auto-pay cards near expiry
- [ ] Pay any remaining manual bills
- [ ] Update Overdue Tracker if anything is late
