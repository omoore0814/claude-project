## Campaign 2 — Sexual Health Light Therapy Launch

**Campaign name:** "Reclaim Your Vitality" (or "Restore" — test both)
**Target devices:** Sexual health light therapy
**Revenue target:** $3,200/month → 8 sessions/month
**Timeline:** Launch April 2026 (sensitivity requires proper groundwork first)

### Target Audience
- Women 35–60 experiencing intimacy, comfort, or sensation changes
- Menopausal / perimenopausal clients
- Clients already doing pelvic floor therapy
- Dr. Nucci weight loss referrals (hormonal concerns overlap)

### Messaging Framework
Keep messaging tasteful, warm, and medically credible.
> "Wellness that goes beyond the surface."
> "Your comfort. Your confidence. Your choice."
> "Private, professional, and genuinely life-changing."

Avoid: clinical coldness, overly explicit language, or anything that feels inappropriate.

### Offer
- **Package:** 8 sessions — $2,795 (regular $3,200 — save $405)
- **Combo package:** Pair with pelvic floor for The Wellness & Intimacy Reset ($3,995)
- **Intro:** Private complimentary consultation (discreet booking option — consider separate booking link labeled "Wellness Consultation")

### Channels
- [ ] Private email to select existing client list (not broad social media blast initially)
- [ ] Discreet in-clinic brochure in treatment rooms and private consultation area
- [ ] Provider verbal mention in appropriate clinical conversations
- [ ] Instagram — educational/awareness content only (no explicit claims)
- [ ] Dr. Nucci cross-referral
- [ ] Consider a private landing page on Shopify / website for discreet inquiries

### Key Consideration
This service requires a higher-trust sales pathway. Clients won't respond to a hard sell. Lead with empathy, education, and a private consultation offer. Never discuss specifics in the waiting area.

### KPIs
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Private consultations booked | 5/month |
| Packages sold | 3/month |
| Session revenue | $3,200/month |
