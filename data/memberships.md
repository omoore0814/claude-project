## Membership Conversion Playbook

### When to Present a Membership
1. When a client books their 2nd treatment
2. When a client purchases a 3+ session package
3. When a client asks about "maintaining" results
4. When a client asks about long-term pricing
5. At the end of every consultation

### How to Present It

**Script:**
> "Before you go — I wanted to mention something that a lot of our clients have found really valuable. We have a membership program that was designed specifically for clients who are as committed to their results as you are. Depending on which tier fits your goals, you could be saving anywhere from $50 to over $1,000 a month. Can I take two minutes to walk you through what's included?"

### Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I'm not sure I'll come enough." | "That's actually why I think Tier 1 is perfect for you — it's just one visit a month and you're already coming in. You'd be saving money and building consistency at the same time." |
| "I need to think about it." | "Of course. Can I email you the comparison so you have it in front of you? And if you decide within the next [X days], I'll waive your enrollment fee / include a welcome gift." |
| "Is there a contract?" | "Tier 1 is completely month-to-month — no commitment at all. You can pause or cancel anytime with 30 days' notice." |
| "I already spend a lot here." | "That's exactly why this makes sense — you're already investing in your results. The membership just makes sure you're getting the best price every time you do." |

## Membership Admin Notes
- Process memberships on a fixed monthly billing date (1st or 15th — choose one and standardize)
- Members receive a digital membership card or note in their client file
- Flag lapsed or paused members for re-engagement monthly
- Never let a membership lapse without a personal outreach call or message
- Review tier performance quarterly — retire underperforming tiers, create seasonal offers
