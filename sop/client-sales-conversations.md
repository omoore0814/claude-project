# SOP: Client Sales Conversations — Medical Esthetics Thunder Bay

> Last updated: 2026-03-19
> Use this SOP for in-person consultations, phone inquiries, and follow-up conversations.

---

## Purpose
To convert inquiries and consultations into booked treatments, packages, or memberships using an ethical, empathy-led, outcome-focused approach.

---

## Core Principles
1. Listen first. Sell second.
2. Uncover the client's goal before recommending a solution.
3. Educate — never pressure.
4. Always suggest the next step.
5. Recommend packages when they genuinely improve outcomes.
6. Reinforce transformation, value, safety, and convenience.

---

## Step 1: Warm Welcome
- Greet the client by name.
- Thank them for coming in / calling / reaching out.
- Set a relaxed, professional tone immediately.
- Offer water, seating, or a moment to settle if in-person.

**Script example:**
> "Welcome in! So glad you're here today. We're going to take really good care of you. Can I grab you some water before we get started?"

---

## Step 2: Discovery — Understand the Goal
Ask open-ended questions to uncover what the client actually wants.

**Key questions:**
- "What's been bothering you most, or what's your main goal today?"
- "How long have you been dealing with this?"
- "Have you tried any treatments before? How did those go?"
- "What would a great result look like for you?"
- "Is there a timeline you have in mind — an event, season, or milestone?"

**Do not:**
- Jump straight into treatment recommendations before listening
- Talk about price before establishing value
- Offer too many options at once

---

## Step 3: Educate & Recommend
Match the client's goal to the best solution. Explain simply, confidently, and benefit-led.

**Framework:**
1. Name the treatment
2. Explain what it does (simple, outcome-focused language)
3. Tell them what results they can expect
4. State how many sessions are typically recommended
5. Introduce the package or plan

**Script example:**
> "Based on what you're describing — that loss of volume and early skin laxity — I'd recommend starting with [treatment]. What it does is [simple explanation]. Most clients see [result] after [X] sessions. We actually have a package designed for exactly this that gives you [benefit] at a savings of [amount]. Would you like me to walk you through what that looks like?"

---

## Step 4: Handle Hesitation
If the client seems uncertain, don't push — explore.

**Common hesitations and responses:**

| Hesitation | Response |
|---|---|
| "I need to think about it." | "Of course — can I ask what's on your mind? I want to make sure you have everything you need to decide." |
| "It seems expensive." | "I completely understand. Let me show you what each session breaks down to — and compare it to the results most clients experience. Many find it's one of the best investments they've made." |
| "I'm not sure if it'll work for me." | "That's a really fair concern. Can I show you some before and afters from clients with similar goals? Our [provider] is also happy to answer any clinical questions before you commit." |
| "I want to check with my partner." | "Absolutely. Would it help if I put together a summary you could share with them? I can also book a complimentary consult for you both together." |

---

## Step 5: Close — Always Suggest the Next Step
Never end a conversation without a clear next action.

**Options:**
- Book the first treatment
- Book a consultation with a provider
- Enroll in a membership
- Purchase a package
- Take a quote / proposal home with a follow-up date

**Script examples:**
> "Would you like to get your first session on the calendar today? We have [day/time] available."
> "I'd love to book you in for a complimentary consultation with [provider] so she can answer your clinical questions and build your plan."

---

## Step 6: Document the Conversation
After every consultation or inquiry:
- Note the client's goal and any hesitations in the booking system
- Flag for follow-up if they didn't book
- Assign a follow-up date (see lead-follow-up SOP)

---

## Add-On / Cross-Sell Guidelines
Only suggest add-ons when:
- The add-on is genuinely complementary to the client's goal
- The timing is right (not mid-payment or when client seems overwhelmed)
- You can explain the benefit clearly in one sentence

**Example:**
> "Since you're doing [treatment], a lot of clients also add [complementary treatment] — it targets [secondary goal] and the two work really well together. Want me to include that in your quote?"

---

## Membership Conversion Touchpoints
Introduce memberships when:
- The client is booking a series of treatments (3+ sessions)
- The client says they want to "maintain" results
- The client asks about long-term pricing
- The client is returning for the second or third visit

**Script:**
> "We actually have a membership that was designed for clients who love [result] and want to keep it going. It includes [treatments] every month and gives you [% discount] on everything else. It works out to [$/month] — would you like me to go over what's included?"
