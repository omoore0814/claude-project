# SOP: Vendor Communication — Medical Esthetics Thunder Bay

> Last updated: 2026-03-19

---

## Purpose
To maintain professional, organized, and proactive communication with all vendors — ensuring timely delivery, accurate billing, strong support, and favorable terms.

---

## Core Principles
- Be professional and concise in all written communication.
- Follow up persistently but politely — never let a vendor go silent.
- Document every interaction, commitment, and next step.
- Flag billing errors, delays, or stock risks immediately.
- Negotiate from a position of knowledge — know your current terms before any renewal.

---

## Vendor Communication Categories

### 1. Ordering & Restocking
Use when placing orders, requesting quotes, or confirming stock availability.

**Steps:**
1. Check current inventory levels before ordering (see inventory SOP).
2. Confirm reorder point has been reached.
3. Send order via preferred channel (portal, email, phone — see vendors.md).
4. Request written confirmation with expected delivery date.
5. Log the order in inventory.md under "Reorder Tracker."
6. Follow up if no confirmation received within 48 hours.

---

### 2. Invoice & Billing Issues
Use when an invoice is incorrect, overdue, or disputed.

**Steps:**
1. Review the invoice against the original order confirmation.
2. Note any discrepancies (amount, quantity, item, date).
3. Contact vendor in writing with specific details (see vendor-email-template.md).
4. Request a corrected invoice with a target turnaround date.
5. Do not pay a disputed invoice until resolved.
6. Log the issue in the Vendor Issues Log in vendors.md.

---

### 3. Device Support & Service Requests
Use when a device is underperforming, requires calibration, or is experiencing technical issues.

**Steps:**
1. Document the issue clearly (what device, what's happening, when it started).
2. Check if the device is under warranty or service contract.
3. Contact the vendor's support line or dedicated rep.
4. Request a service ticket number and estimated response time.
5. Follow up every 48 hours until resolved.
6. Log downtime and impact on revenue for future vendor negotiations.

---

### 4. Training & Onboarding Requests
Use when a new device is delivered or a new team member needs training.

**Steps:**
1. Confirm training is included in the purchase/lease agreement.
2. Request available training dates in writing.
3. Confirm who will attend (provider name, role).
4. Follow up with a written confirmation of the scheduled session.
5. Document training completion date in vendors.md.

---

### 5. Vendor Follow-Up (No Response)
Use when a vendor has not responded within the expected timeframe.

**Follow-up timeline:**
- Day 1: Initial request sent
- Day 3: First follow-up (email or phone)
- Day 5: Second follow-up — escalate to account manager or supervisor if needed
- Day 7: Final follow-up — flag internally and assess vendor reliability

**Script:**
> "Hi [Name], just following up on my message from [date]. I haven't heard back yet and wanted to confirm [request]. Please let me know by [date] so we can move forward. Thank you."

---

## Documentation Requirements
After every vendor interaction:
- Update vendors.md (contact, issue, resolution, next step)
- Log any billing disputes in the Vendor Issues Log
- Note any delays that affected clinic operations

---

## Vendor Review Schedule
- **Monthly:** Review open issues and follow-up items
- **Quarterly:** Review pricing and compare against alternatives
- **Annually:** Review all contracts for renewal, renegotiation, or replacement
