# GOTO COMMUNICATION SYSTEM

## Objective
Convert all inbound calls and texts into booked consultations or treatments within 10 minutes.

---

## ACCESS RULES

- GoTo access is restricted to authorized staff only
- No credentials stored in GitHub
- Access managed via password manager (1Password)

---

## LEAD RESPONSE STANDARD

ALL leads must be:
- responded to within 10 minutes
- acknowledged warmly
- guided toward booking

---

## TEXT RESPONSE STRUCTURE

1. Acknowledge
2. Clarify
3. Recommend
4. Close

---

## TEMPLATE RESPONSE

Hi [Name] 😊

Thanks so much for reaching out! I'd love to help with this.

Can I ask what your main goal is right now so we can guide you to the best treatment?

We can absolutely help with this, and the best next step would be a consultation so we can build a plan tailored to you.

Would you like me to book you in this week?

---

## SALES RULES

- Always move toward booking
- Never give too many options
- Recommend best treatment confidently
- Offer packages if appropriate
- Suggest add-ons when relevant

---

## MISSED CALL SYSTEM

Every missed call must:
1. receive a callback within 5–10 minutes
2. receive a text message

TEXT:

Hi [Name], we just missed your call! 😊

How can we help today? I'd love to get you booked in or answer any questions you have.

---

## FOLLOW-UP SYSTEM

If no response:
- follow up at 24 hours
- follow up at 72 hours

---

## BOOKING PUSH

Always say:
"Let's get you booked in"

NOT:
"Let me know if you want to book"

---

## KPI TARGETS

- response time: under 10 minutes
- booking rate: 60%+
- missed lead rate: 0%
