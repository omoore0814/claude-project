# SOP: Lead Follow-Up — Medical Esthetics Thunder Bay

> Last updated: 2026-03-19

---

## Purpose
To convert new inquiries into booked consultations and treatments through a fast, warm, and consistent follow-up system. Speed and personalization are the two highest-leverage variables in lead conversion.

---

## Core Principles
- Respond to every lead within 2 hours during business hours.
- Personalize every message — never send generic copy-paste replies.
- Leads go cold fast — urgency is critical in the first 24 hours.
- Never give up after one or two messages. A structured sequence converts more.
- Always include a specific call to action.

---

## Lead Sources
Track where every lead comes from:

- Other (specify)

## Follow-Up Sequence

### Step 1 — Immediate Response (within 2 hours)
Acknowledge the inquiry, show genuine interest, and invite them to the next step.

**Channel:** Same as inquiry (DM → DM, email → email, phone → phone)

**Goal:** Establish connection and book consultation or answer initial questions.

See client-reply-template.md for message templates.

---

### Step 2 — Follow-Up #1 (24–48 hours if no response)
A short, warm check-in.

**Script:**
> "Hi [Name]! Just wanted to make sure my message came through. I'd love to help you with [goal they mentioned]. We have a few openings this week — would any of these times work for a quick consultation? [Day/Time options]"

---

### Step 3 — Follow-Up #2 (4–5 days if still no response)
Provide value — share a relevant outcome, offer, or piece of education.

**Script:**
> "Hi [Name], I know life gets busy! I wanted to share something that might be helpful — [one-sentence result or fact about the treatment they inquired about]. If you have any questions or want to come in for a complimentary consult, I'm here. No pressure at all — just want to make sure you have the info you need."

---

### Step 4 — Follow-Up #3 (7–10 days if still no response)
Final check-in. Keep it short and light.

**Script:**
> "Hi [Name] — last check-in from me! If [goal] is still on your radar, we'd love to help. If the timing isn't right, no worries at all — just reach out whenever you're ready. We're always here."

---

### Step 5 — Re-Engagement (30 days later, if no response to prior sequence)
Add to a monthly email or DM list for re-engagement. Do not bombard.

## Rules
- Never argue with or pressure a lead.
- Never discount in the first message.
- If asked about price, explain value first, then give price.
- Flag leads with high intent (e.g. specific treatment requested, price asked) for immediate priority.
- Assign all leads to a specific team member — no lead should be "everyone's responsibility."
