# SOP: Missed Bills Prevention — Medical Esthetics Thunder Bay

> Last updated: 2026-03-19

---

## Purpose
To eliminate missed or late bill payments through a structured tracking and reminder system. Late payments damage vendor relationships, incur fees, and add unnecessary stress to operations.

---

## Core Principles
- Every bill must be logged in bills.md before it is due.
- Assign a responsible person to review the bill tracker every 1st and 15th of the month.
- Never rely on memory alone.
- Treat a missed bill as a process failure, not a personal failure — fix the system.

---

## Step 1 — Add All Bills to the Tracker
When a new bill or recurring charge is identified:
1. Open data/bills.md.
2. Add the vendor/bill name, amount, due date, payment method reference, and auto-pay status.
3. Note whether it auto-drafts or requires manual payment.
4. Save and commit the update.

---

## Step 2 — Set Calendar Reminders
For every bill that is NOT on auto-pay:
- Set a calendar reminder 5 days before the due date.
- Set a second reminder 1 day before the due date.
- Calendar invites should include: bill name, amount, payment method.

---

## Step 3 — Bi-Monthly Review (1st and 15th)
On the 1st and 15th of every month:
1. Open bills.md.
2. Review all bills due in the next 15 days.
3. Confirm auto-pay bills have been processed (check bank statement).
4. Action any manual payments needed.
5. Update the "Status" column for all reviewed bills.
6. Flag any bill that is overdue and move it to the Overdue Tracker.

---

## Step 4 — Handle Overdue Bills
If a bill is overdue:
1. Log it in the Overdue Tracker in bills.md immediately.
2. Contact the vendor or service provider to explain and arrange payment.
3. Pay the bill as soon as possible.
4. Note any late fees incurred.
5. Investigate why it was missed and update the system to prevent recurrence.

---

## Step 5 — End-of-Month Reconciliation
On the last business day of each month:
1. Review the Monthly Bill Summary table in bills.md.
2. Confirm total bills paid vs. total due.
3. Note any outstanding items.
4. Add the totals to the KPI dashboard (kpis.md).

---

## Accountability Assignment

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| [Owner / Manager] | Final accountability for all bills |
| [Admin / Operations] | Day-to-day tracker updates and reminders |

---

## Common Failure Points & Fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Bill not added to tracker when signed | Add new bill to bills.md same day contract is signed |
| Auto-pay card expired | Review card expiry dates quarterly; update before expiry |
| Staff forgot to pay manual bill | Add calendar reminders; escalate if missed |
| Invoice received but not matched to bill | Match invoices to the tracker within 24 hours of receipt |
| Vendor changed due date or amount | Update bills.md immediately when notified |
